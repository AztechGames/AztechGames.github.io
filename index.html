About

I've worked across Unity development, game design and product management. At my own studio, Aztech Games, I ran product development end to end: a team of ten, publisher relations with Rollic, Moonee and Lion Studios, and the strategy behind what we built next.

I like decisions backed by data and a technical understanding of what's possible, because that's how player experience and product performance improve together. I also teach — more than 1000 hours in person and online, for universities, private institutions and our own academy.

Away from the screen: paragliding, scuba diving, camping — and more games.
