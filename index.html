About me

I've worked across Unity development, game design and product management. At my studio, Aztech Games, I ran product development end to end: a team of ten, publisher relations and the strategy behind what we built next.

I make decisions with data and a solid technical understanding, because that's how player experience and product performance improve together. I also love teaching — more than 1000 hours for universities, private institutions and our own academy.

Off-screen: paragliding, scuba diving, camping — and, of course, more games.
